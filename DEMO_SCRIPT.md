# 90-second Hackathon Demo Script — Pip's Dream Desk Phase 2

**0:00–0:12 · Meet Pip**  
“This is Pip, a tiny skull baby and the Midnight Nursery's Junior Dream Engineer. We wanted Pip's portfolio to feel like a real little world you could visit, not just read.” Click Pip to make him wave.

**0:12–0:30 · Three inventions**  
“First, Pip's music box. I can play his lullaby or build my own short tune with these six notes.” Tap C–E–G and replay. “His lantern changes between sunset, lavender moonlight and midnight blue.” Switch moods. “Dream delivery has three routes and different packages.” Launch a letter.

**0:30–1:02 · Signature feature**  
“The most personal part is Build a Little Dream.” Pick Adventurous, Cloud Garden and Baby Dragon. “These choices make a complete little bedtime story locally, without any AI API or sign-in.” Generate it; show the story, picture and Pip's note. Click Another Dream, then Copy Story.

**1:02–1:20 · Personality and exploration**  
“There's also a five-letter sleepy mailroom toy, a three-part scrolling origin story, tiny cursor sparkles and a secret moon.” Solve a game round and knock on the moon five times.

**1:20–1:30 · Build quality**  
“It's made with just HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, supports phones, keyboard access, muted audio and reduced motion, and deploys directly to GitHub Pages. Thanks for visiting Pip's desk!”

Use real interactions in the demo; do not claim a guestbook, hit counter, external AI, screenshot export, or unverified bonus points.
