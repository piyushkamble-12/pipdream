CHAPTER ONE · A STAR FALLS ASLEEP

It began with a yawn.

One quiet evening, a star fell asleep before it could shine. I found it curled up inside my nightcap, warm as a freshly baked cookie. I didn't want to wake it.

A song made of small things.

So I made a lullaby from an old button, a ribbon, and three humming moonbeams. The star smiled in its sleep. That's when I learned that small things can make the world feel safe.
