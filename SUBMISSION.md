## Project description

Meet Pip, the Midnight Nursery's tiny Junior Dream Engineer. Visitors enter Pip's bedtime workshop, where they can compose a miniature six-note music-box tune, give a lantern three different lighting moods, and deliver a dream package along one of three illustrated routes. The signature experience, **Build a Little Dream**, turns three personal choices into a charming custom story with a drawn dream scene, Pip's message, and a downloadable PNG or copyable keepsake. There is also a five-letter Sleepy Mailroom toy, three scroll-revealed origin chapters, a smiling Pip, and a secret moon waiting to be discovered.

## Three required portfolio inventions

1. **Lullaby Lab:** Original playable Web Audio lullaby; build/replay/clear up to 12 custom notes, with a global sound toggle.
2. **Moonbeam Lantern:** Warm Sunset, Moonlit Lavender, and Midnight Blue change the website and lantern preview; choice saved locally where allowed.
3. **Dream Delivery Express:** Choose a destination (Moon Nursery, Cloud Garden, Starlit Forest) and package (courage, calm, wonder); watch a real envelope follow the corresponding SVG route, then read Pip's delivery note.

## Signature visitor experience

**Build a Little Dream:** Select feeling + destination + companion; generates one of 27 deterministic local story combinations. Interactive illustrated card, browser-native PNG export, and story copy action (clipboard or selectable fallback). No AI service, user account, or backend.

## Original portfolio and personality

Pip is a gentle skull baby who designs dreams as the Junior Dream Engineer at the Midnight Nursery. The site contains an introduction, three showcase projects, and three short bedtime origin chapters. It includes a hidden five-knock moon, responsive interface, optional pointer sparkle effect, gentle click chimes with mute, and a five-part sorting game.

## Stack and implementation

Semantic HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Web Audio API, SVG motion paths, `IntersectionObserver`, and localStorage. GitHub Pages static hosting. No build system or API keys. Built with keyboard/touch support, responsive layout and reduced-motion handling.
